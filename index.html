THE MECHANISM

What happens beneath the glass?

A modern capacitive touchscreen does not need a button to know where you touched. It senses a tiny electrical change.

01

A transparent sensor grid

Transparent conductive electrodes form a grid beneath the glass. The controller continually scans this grid.

02

Your finger changes capacitance

Your finger affects the local electric field, changing the capacitance measured around that area.

03
+X 08Y 05

The controller finds the position

The controller analyses the sensor readings and estimates the touch location, then sends coordinates to the phone.
